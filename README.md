# Vendor Performance Analysis
Python (Pandas, SQLite3, SciPy), Power BI, SQL
### This project transformed raw data into a profit-optimization blueprint: rebalancing vendor partnerships, unlocking inventory capital, and aligning pricing with market demand. The result? A scalable framework for sustainable profitability.


The Vendor Performance Analysis project is a data-driven initiative designed to optimize profitability in retail/wholesale operations. Leveraging ERP, sales, and inventory data, it identifies inefficiencies in pricing, vendor performance, bulk purchasing, and inventory turnover. The outcome is an actionable analytics framework that empowers strategic decision-making for profit growth.

The retail business faced critical challenges:
Profit leakage from inefficient pricing and promotions.
Blind spots in vendor performance (e.g., 65.7% of purchases concentrated in top 10 vendors).
$2.71M trapped in unsold inventory due to poor turnover.
Extreme profit variance (low-performing vendors had 41.6% margins vs. top vendors’ 31.2% but struggled with volume).
The goal was clear: transform data into strategies to boost margins, reduce costs, and mitigate supply chain risks.


## Project Road Map

Define Business Problem
Analyzed business requirements (BRD) to scope pain points: pricing, vendor reliance, inventory inefficiency.
Data Preparation
Explored/merged database tables; cleaned data (e.g., filtered negative profits and zero-sales entries).
Analysis & Insights
Used Jupyter Notebook for EDA, statistical testing, and solving research questions.
Visualization
Built interactive Power BI dashboards for vendor scoring and inventory alerts.
Reporting
Synthesized findings into strategic recommendations.


## Key Questions Tackled & Findings

Which brands need pricing/promotion adjustments?
Identified 198 high-margin, low-sales brands (e.g., Crown Royal Apple: 89.8% margin).
Action: Targeted promotions to drive volume without eroding margins.
Who are the top vendors, and are we over-reliant?
Top 10 vendors drove 65.7% of purchases (e.g., Diageo North America).
Risk: Supply chain vulnerability.
Action: Diversify partnerships to reduce dependency.
Does bulk purchasing lower costs?
Bulk orders (500+ units) slashed unit costs by 72% ($10.78/unit vs. $39.06 for small orders).
Action: Negotiate bulk discounts while optimizing holding costs.
Which vendors have slow inventory turnover?
Flagged vendors like Alisa Carr Beverages (turnover: 0.62) holding $722K unsold stock.
Action: Clearance sales or stock-level adjustments.
Why do low-performing vendors have higher margins?
Hypothesis testing confirmed: Low-volume vendors had 41.6% margins vs. top vendors’ 31.2%.
Root cause: Pricing inefficiencies/poor market reach.
Action: Revise pricing and boost marketing for underperformers.
